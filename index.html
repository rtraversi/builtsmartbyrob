Private Managed Hosting

AI and software can save your business hours every week — but if you handle confidential information, where that data lives and travels matters as much as what the tools do. Private Managed Hosting is built for businesses that can't afford to get this wrong.

Whether I build you a client portal, an automation, or both, every client gets their own private, isolated system — your own database, application, and AI key, kept completely separate from every other client. Your data is connected to AI services under enterprise terms, and never routed through consumer platforms. The result: the same productivity gains you'd get from popular AI tools, with a defensible answer to the question "where does our client data go?"

Your Private Client Portal

The centerpiece of most setups is a private, branded portal your team logs into to run the day-to-day — all on your own isolated system. Think of it as your firm's secure command center: clients, documents, and AI-powered tools in one place, accessible to your staff and no one else.

  • Client records — searchable client cards, linked to every document and upload
  • Secure document intake — staff upload and process sensitive files right in the portal
  • AI-powered tools — read, check, translate, and sort documents in seconds, tailored to your practice
  • Staff logins & roles — each team member gets their own secure access
  • Your branding — your colors, your logo, your firm's name throughout

Why This Matters

Most popular AI and software tools route your data through shared servers, retain it for years, or use it to train future models. For attorneys, healthcare providers, financial advisors, HR teams, and anyone bound by confidentiality obligations, that's not an inconvenience — it's a liability. The fix isn't avoiding AI; it's running it on infrastructure you control, under terms that forbid training and guarantee deletion.

For attorneys: three 2026 federal rulings (Morgan, Warner, and Heppner) draw the line not at "legal AI vs. general AI," but at consumer accounts vs. enterprise terms — exactly what this setup provides. Read the full case-law analysis →

Built Secure by Default

Per-client isolation, encryption in transit and at rest, no AI training on your data, and your choice of fully managed or self-hosted infrastructure — every system is built to a standard you can hand to an auditor. The full breakdown, in plain English, lives on its own page.

Office Visit Tracker

Why Automation?

Work Smarter, Not Harder

Running a small business means wearing a lot of hats — and too often, those hats involve repetitive, time-consuming tasks that pull you away from the work that actually grows your business.

That's where custom automation comes in — built around your exact process, not bent to fit someone else's. Instead of paying a premium for big-box software that still only does part of what you need, you get a workflow shaped to the way your business actually runs: less wasted time, less wasted money, and nothing you're paying for but don't use. Here's what that looks like in the real world:

⚖️

Immigration Law

A new client submits their information online. A case file is created in your case management system. A welcome email goes out immediately. The attorney has a task waiting for them — all before anyone has touched a keyboard.

🏠

Real Estate

New listing inquiries route to the right agent automatically. Follow-up sequences trigger based on client activity. The right documents reach the right people the moment they're needed — with zero manual intervention.

👪

Family Law

A new matter opens and the intake details flow straight into a client record. Court dates and filing deadlines land on the calendar with reminders attached. Documents organize themselves by case from day one — so nothing slips during an already stressful time for the client.

📜

Estate Planning

Client questionnaires turn into organized files automatically. Signing appointments trigger the right document packets. Periodic review reminders go out on schedule — so wills, trusts, and powers of attorney stay current without anyone tracking it by hand.

The Next Level

Pair Automation with AI — and Your Workflows Can Think

Instead of just moving data from point A to point B, your workflows can now reason, interpret, and act on the content itself. This is where the real time savings happen.

  • 📋 Review completed USCIS application packages, cross-check every field against requirements, and flag issues for attorney review — before they go out the door.
  • 📬 When a USCIS notice arrives with a biometric appointment date, read the document, extract the key details, and fire a reminder text to your client automatically — so nothing falls through the cracks.
  • 📧 Incoming client emails can be analyzed, categorized, and prioritized before a human even reads them — turning a flooded inbox into a managed queue.

These are exactly the kinds of tasks that eat up hours of staff time every week — and exactly the kinds of tasks that automation handles better, faster, and more consistently than any manual process can.

The best part? The same capabilities that large enterprises pay hundreds of thousands of dollars for are now accessible to any small business — at a fraction of the cost.
