Brightwater Law Group does not exist. The firm, the client, every account, institution, and dollar figure on this page were generated for demonstration. Nothing here belongs to a real client, and no real client's records were reshaped to produce it.

Financial discovery shows up across practice areas — divorce, probate and estate administration, elder law, business litigation — and it always starts the same way: a production of bank statements that has to be confirmed complete before anyone can rely on it. This automation ingests the entire production, reads what each statement prints about itself, and surfaces exactly what's missing — coverage gaps, incomplete statements, and balances that don't carry from one month to the next — before anyone reads a single transaction line by line.
