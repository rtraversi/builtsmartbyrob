1. Who we are

This website and the IurisIQ client portal are run by BSBR Holdings, LLC. We do business as Built Smart by Rob. In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" mean BSBR Holdings, LLC. "You" means anyone who visits our website or uses a portal we run.

We are based in Raleigh, North Carolina, in the United States.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers two things:

  • Our website, builtsmartbyrob.com.
  • IurisIQ, the case management and client portal we build for law firms. It covers every IurisIQ portal that we host and run for a law firm.

In IurisIQ, the law firm is our customer. The firm decides what client data goes into its portal. We store and process that data for the firm, and only to run the portal for the firm. If you are a client of a law firm that uses IurisIQ, the firm's own privacy policy also applies to you.

Some firms choose to run IurisIQ on their own accounts instead of ours. In that case, the firm controls the data, and we cannot see it unless the firm gives us access.

3. Data we collect

On our website

  • Contact form. Your first and last name, email address, business name, the service you are interested in, and your message.
  • Reviews. If we invite you to review our work, we collect your rating, your review, and the name you want shown with it.
  • Basic technical data. Our web host keeps standard server logs. These include your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit, and the time of each visit.

Our website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. We do not collect your precise location.

In IurisIQ

  • Account data. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and login details for law firm staff and for clients who use the portal.
  • Contact data. Contact details a firm enters for its clients and other people in a case, such as family members or sponsors.
  • Case data. Information about a legal matter. For immigration cases, this can include dates and places of birth, immigration history, and government ID numbers, such as USCIS receipt numbers and A-Numbers.
  • Documents. Files that a firm or its clients upload, such as forms, letters, and evidence. These can contain financial data, such as tax returns or pay stubs. They can also contain health data, such as a medical exam form.
  • Messages and tasks. Notes, messages, and tasks created in the portal.
  • Case status data. When a firm tracks a USCIS receipt number, we get the case status and case history for that number from USCIS.
  • Billing data. Invoices and payment records, if the firm uses billing features. Card numbers are handled by the firm's payment processor. We never see or store full card numbers.
  • Technical data. Login times, IP addresses, and a record of key actions, kept for security.

IurisIQ does not collect your precise location, and it does not read your phone contacts.

4. How we use data

We use data only for these purposes:

  • To answer your questions and give you a quote.
  • To run the IurisIQ portal for the law firm, including storing files and showing case status.
  • To look up case status from USCIS when a firm asks us to track a receipt number.
  • To keep accounts secure, stop misuse, and fix problems.
  • To send messages about your account or service, such as login links and security notices.
  • To follow the law and respond to valid legal requests.

De-identified data. We do not create, share, or sell de-identified, anonymized, or pseudonymized data. That means data with names removed is still not shared or sold.

No AI training. Some IurisIQ features use AI to read documents or draft text. These features run on commercial terms that forbid the AI provider from training on your data. We never use your data to train AI models.

No advertising. We never use your data for advertising or marketing to others.

5. We do not sell data

We do not sell your data. We never have, and we never will. This includes sales for money and any other kind of trade or payment. We also do not rent your data or trade it.

6. Who receives data

Third parties may not use or disclose your information, including de-identified information, without your active consent. "Active consent" means you take a clear step to agree, such as clicking a button or signing a form. We never treat silence as consent.

We use a small number of service providers to run our website and IurisIQ. They act only on our instructions. Each one is bound by the terms of this policy. They may use your data only to provide their service to us. They may not use it for their own purposes.

Service providers we use

  • Netlify hosts our website and receives contact form messages.
  • Cloudflare hosts IurisIQ, stores uploaded files, and blocks bots.
  • Supabase hosts IurisIQ databases and our review records.
  • Backblaze stores encrypted backup copies of IurisIQ files.
  • Resend sends account and notice emails.
  • Anthropic runs AI features in IurisIQ, under terms that forbid training on your data.
  • iLovePDF converts and compresses PDF files when a user asks.
  • attachmentAV scans uploaded files for viruses.

U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS)

When a law firm tracks a case in IurisIQ, we send the USCIS receipt number to the USCIS Case Status API. We send only the receipt number. USCIS sends back the case status. USCIS already has this case, because you or your firm filed it.

Services a law firm chooses to connect

A law firm can choose to connect IurisIQ to other services it uses. These include Google, Microsoft, or Dropbox for calendars and files. They also include QuickBooks or FreshBooks for accounting, and Stripe, Payload, or Gravity Legal for payments. Data goes to these services only if the firm turns on the connection. Each one is the firm's own account, under the firm's own agreement with that service.

Legal requests

We may disclose data if the law requires it, such as for a valid subpoena or court order. When the law allows, we will tell the affected law firm or person first. This gives them a chance to object.

7. Your sharing choices

You decide what you share with us and with your law firm. Here is what each choice means.

  • Contact form. You choose what to write. The benefit is that we can answer you. The risk is low, but please do not send confidential details through the form.
  • USCIS case tracking. Your firm sends only your receipt number to USCIS. The benefit is fast, automatic status updates. The limit is that the status is only as current as USCIS's own records.
  • Connected services. The benefit is less double entry for your firm. The risk is that a copy of the data then also lives in that service, under its own policy. The limit is that we cannot delete data from a service the firm connected. The firm must do that in the other service.
  • Uploading documents. The benefit is that your firm has what it needs for your case. The risk is that a document can hold more data than the case needs. If you can, cover or remove what is not needed before you upload.

You can change your mind. Ask your law firm or contact us, and we will help you stop sharing.

8. How sharing can affect others

Legal cases often involve other people. An immigration case can include data about a spouse, children, parents, or a financial sponsor. When you share a document, you may also share data about them.

Please share only what you have the right to share. If you are unsure, ask your law firm. We protect data about other people in the same way we protect yours.

9. How we protect data

  • All traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Stored data and files are encrypted.
  • Each law firm gets its own separate database and storage. Firms never share a database.
  • Only people the firm approves can log in. Access rules are enforced inside the database.
  • Secret keys stay on our servers. They are never sent to your browser.
  • Uploaded files are scanned for viruses.

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot promise that data will never be exposed. But we work hard to prevent it, and we will tell you if it happens.

10. Data breaches

If we learn that your data was accessed without permission, we will:

  • Tell the affected law firm within 72 hours of confirming the breach.
  • Tell affected people as soon as possible, by email, either directly or through their law firm.
  • Explain what happened, what data was involved, and what we are doing about it.
  • Tell you what steps to take, such as changing your password or watching your accounts.
  • Notify government agencies when the law requires it.

11. How long we keep data

  • Contact form messages: 2 years after our last contact with you. Then we delete them.
  • Review invitations: they expire after 30 days if not used.
  • Published reviews: until you ask us to remove them.
  • IurisIQ data: as long as the law firm uses IurisIQ. Law firms are often required to keep client files for years, so the firm decides how long to keep each case.
  • Deleted files in IurisIQ: they stay in the trash for 30 days. Then we erase them for good.
  • When a firm stops using IurisIQ: we give the firm a copy of its data. Then we delete it within 90 days.
  • Backup copies: erased within 90 days after the original is deleted.

Dormant accounts

We do not delete an account just because no one has logged in. The firm may still need the case file. A dormant account's data is kept until the firm closes the account or stops using IurisIQ. Then we delete it within 90 days. You can ask us to delete a dormant account at any time.

12. How to delete your data

You can ask us to permanently delete your data at any time. Here is how:

  • Email rob@builtsmartbyrob.com with the subject "Delete my data."
  • Tell us your name and the email address linked to your data.
  • If your data is in a law firm's portal, tell us the name of the firm.

What happens next:

  • We confirm we got your request within 5 business days.
  • We may ask you to confirm your identity, so no one else can delete your data.
  • We permanently delete your data within 30 days after we confirm your identity.
  • Backup copies are erased within 90 days.
  • We email you when the deletion is done.

For data in a law firm's portal, we work with the firm to delete it. Sometimes a law or court rule requires the firm to keep a record. If so, we will tell you what must be kept and why. We will delete everything else.

13. How to close your account

  • Clients of a law firm: ask your firm to close your portal account, or email us. Your login stops working right away.
  • Law firm staff: your firm's portal admin can remove your account at any time.
  • Law firms: send us written notice to end your service. We give you a full copy of your data first. Then we delete it within 90 days.

After an account is closed, its data is deleted as described in section 12.

14. Health information

Some immigration cases need health records, such as a medical exam form. IurisIQ does not ask for health data on its own. It holds health data only when a firm or client uploads it.

  • To download it: sign in to your portal and download the file. Or ask your firm or us for a copy.
  • To send it safely: upload it through the portal. The connection is encrypted. Please do not send health records by regular email.
  • To dispose of it: ask your firm or us to delete it. We erase it as described in section 12. If you downloaded a copy, delete it from your device and empty your trash. For paper copies, use a cross-cut shredder.

15. Your privacy rights

Everyone has these rights with us, no matter where you live:

  • Know: ask what data we have about you and how we use it.
  • Copy: get a copy of your data in a common format.
  • Correct: fix data that is wrong.
  • Delete: have your data deleted, as described above.
  • No sale: we already never sell data, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Fair treatment: we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We honor them for everyone. To use any right, email us. We respond within 30 days. You may also use an authorized agent to make a request for you.

16. Children

Our website and IurisIQ are not meant for children to use. We do not knowingly collect data from a child under 13 directly. A law firm may enter data about a child as part of a family's case. That data gets the same protection as everyone else's. A parent or guardian can ask us to see or delete it.

17. If our company changes hands

Our company could be sold, merge with another company, or close. If that happens:

  • We will email you at least 30 days before any of your data moves to a new owner.
  • The new owner must follow this policy for the data it receives.
  • Before the move, you can ask us to delete your data or give you a copy.
  • If the new owner wants to change this policy, it must get your active consent first.
  • If we close, we give each law firm a copy of its data. Then we delete all data within 90 days.

18. Changes to this policy

For small changes, like fixing typos or updating a contact detail, we update this page and the date at the top.

For any change to how we collect, use, share, or keep your data, we will:

  • Email you a plain-language summary of what changed and why.
  • Ask you to agree by clicking a button in the email or in the portal, or by replying "I agree."
  • Not apply the change to your data until you agree.

If you do not agree, the old policy keeps covering your data. You can also close your account, and we will delete your data. We list every change in the history below.
